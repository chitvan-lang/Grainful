GRAINFUL - HOMEPAGE ON THE HIMON TEMPLATE (PREVIEW)
==================================================

HOW TO VIEW
Open index.html in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Keep the assets folder next to it.

WHAT THIS IS
- The Grainful homepage rebuilt on the Himon template (himon.framer.website): its layout, Geist type and section
  patterns, in colours taken from the Grainful logo (red, grain gold, cream glow) and the grainful.in green.
- All text comes from grainful.in. Obvious spelling slips were corrected; nothing was invented.
- Only the homepage is built. Menu, buttons and footer links open the existing pages on grainful.in until the inner
  pages are rebuilt.

FOLDER
index.html        the homepage
assets/css        styling
assets/js         header, mobile menu, product chooser, reviews slider, scroll reveals
assets/img        photos from grainful.in (WebP) and the Grainful logo
assets/fonts      Geist and Geist Mono (SIL Open Font License, see GEIST-LICENSE.txt)
